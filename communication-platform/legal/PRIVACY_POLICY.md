# Privacy Policy — Persephone's Basket (FarmConnect)

**Effective date:** August 18, 2026

Persephone's Basket ("we", "us") builds vineyard microclimate sensors and the
FarmConnect app. This policy explains what the app and our service collect, why,
and the choices you have. We keep data collection to what the product needs to
work; we do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Who this covers

This policy applies to the FarmConnect iOS app and the backend service it talks
to. If you received sensor hardware from us, it also covers the readings those
sensors send to your farm's account.

## What we collect

**Account information.** Your email address and a password (stored only as a
salted hash — we never store your password in readable form), and a display
name if you provide one. Used to sign you in and to associate you with your
farm.

**Farm and sensor data.** The farm you belong to, the sensor devices assigned
to it, and the readings those devices report (temperature, humidity, and
related environmental measurements), including a device's location if you place
it on your vineyard map. Used to show you your data and to compute the weather
and disease-risk information in the app.

**Approximate location (optional).** If you choose to set your vineyard's
position or use "current location," we use your device's location once to place
your farm for local weather. We do not track your location in the background.
You can decline this and enter your location manually.

**Photos you choose to share (optional).** If you attach a photo to a message
to the in-app assistant, that image is uploaded to answer your request. We do
not access your photo library otherwise.

**Support and diagnostic information.** Basic logs needed to operate the service
(for example, error records and sensor-health checks). We use these to keep the
service working and to alert us when a sensor reports abnormal readings.

We do **not** collect contacts, browsing history, or advertising identifiers,
and the app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

## How we use your information

- To provide the app: authenticate you, show your farm's sensor data, and
  compute weather and disease-risk estimates.
- To operate and protect the service: detect malfunctioning sensors, prevent
  abuse, and fix problems.
- To communicate with you: send password-reset codes and important service
  notices to your email.

We do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioral
advertising.

## Service providers we use

We rely on a small set of processors to run the service. They handle data only
to provide their function to us:

- **Railway** — application and database hosting.
- **Open-Meteo** — weather data. We send approximate coordinates to retrieve
  local forecast and historical weather; we do not send your identity.
- **OpenRouter / OpenAI** — powers the in-app assistant. Messages (and any photo
  you attach) you send to the assistant are processed to generate a reply.
- **Apple Push Notification service** — to deliver notifications you enable.
- **Email delivery provider** — to send password-reset and service emails.
- **Object storage (Cloudflare R2)** — to store images you upload.

## Disease-risk and agronomic information

The app's weather and disease-risk estimates are **decision support, not a spray
recommendation**. They are estimates from published models and may be wrong. The
product label is the legal authority on any pesticide use, and you should
confirm with Cornell NEWA and a licensed advisor before acting. We are not
responsible for crop decisions made from this information.

## Data retention

We keep account and farm data for as long as your account is active. Sensor
readings are retained to provide season-over-season history. When you delete
your account, we delete or de-identify your personal data within 30 days, except
where we must retain records to comply with law.

## Your choices and rights

- **Access and correction:** contact us to access or correct your information.
- **Deletion:** you can request deletion of your account and personal data at
  the email below. Depending on your region (e.g., EU/UK GDPR, California
  CCPA/CPRA), you may have additional rights to access, port, or delete your
  data and to object to certain processing; we honor these requests.
- **Location and notifications:** you control these in iOS Settings at any time.

## Children

FarmConnect is a tool for commercial and hobby grape growers and is not directed
to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective
date, and significant changes will be communicated in the app or by email.
