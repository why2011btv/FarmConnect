## Description (≤4000)
Persephone's Basket turns your vineyard's own microclimate into clear, block-by-block insight you can act on.

Pair it with Persephone's Basket sensors placed among your vines, and the app shows what's actually happening in each block — not a regional average from miles away.

WHAT YOU GET

Live conditions, by block
See temperature, humidity, and soil moisture from every sensor node through the day, laid out on a map of your own vineyard.

Disease risk you can understand
The app runs published grape-disease models used across the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic — black rot, powdery and downy mildew, Phomopsis, and Botrytis — against each block's real conditions. Every risk comes with a plain-language reason and short, practical scouting and canopy tips.

Built around your season
Set your bloom date and the app tracks growing degree days and the critical fruit-protection window, so timing fits where your vines actually are.

Harvest log
Record Brix, titratable acidity, and pH as fruit ripens, and watch the trend on a simple chart.

We watch your sensors, too
If a node drifts far from nearby weather or goes quiet, our team is alerted — so you're not trusting a bad reading.

DECISION SUPPORT, NOT A PRESCRIPTION
The disease-risk estimates help you scout and plan. They are not spray instructions. The product label is the legal authority on any pesticide use — always follow it, and check Cornell NEWA and a licensed advisor before acting.

GET STARTED
Sign in, enter the access code that came with your sensors, and your farm's data is there. No sensors yet? Get in touch about bringing Persephone's Basket to your vineyard.

Made in the Northeast, for growers who want to know their own ground.
